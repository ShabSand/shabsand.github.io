Chainspark Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Publisher: SHABSAND. Contact: orbechar1@gmail.com

Chainspark is a one-tap chain-reaction puzzle game. We designed it to need as little data as possible.

The iOS app (App Store)

The iOS app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data, and it does not track you. It has no account system, no advertising SDK and no analytics service. Your progress, settings and puzzle results are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage, and are deleted when you delete the app or choose "Reset progress" in Settings.

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or any other device data. It works entirely offline.

The web version (browser / PWA)

WhatDetails
Progress and settingsStored in your browser's local storage on your device only.
Gameplay statisticsAnonymous gameplay events (for example "level 12 cleared in 4 taps") are kept on your device with a random install identifier that is not linked to you. They are not sent anywhere unless a statistics endpoint is enabled; if we ever enable one, you will be asked first and can decline.
AdvertisingThe current web version shows only clearly labelled simulated test ads that load nothing from third parties. If real advertising is introduced, this policy will be updated and, where required, you will be asked for consent before any ad network receives data.
Challenge linksA link you share contains only the puzzle identifier, your tap count and your tap sequence. It never contains your name or any identifier.

Children

Chainspark is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We knowingly collect no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's site data, removes everything stored locally. For any question, write to the address above.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting data (for example optional analytics or real advertising), this page will describe exactly what, why and how to opt out, and the app's privacy details on the App Store will be updated to match.
