Chainspark Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Publisher: SHABSAND. Contact: orbechar1@gmail.com

Chainspark is a one-tap chain-reaction puzzle game. We designed it to need as little data as possible.

The iOS app (App Store)

We do not run any server that receives your data, and the app has no account system. Your progress, settings, Embers balance and puzzle results are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage, and are deleted when you delete the app or choose "Reset progress" in Settings. The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

From version 0.4.0 the iOS app shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To deliver, measure and limit ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process the following directly from your device:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow trackingThird-party advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention
Advertising data and ad interactions (ads shown, taps on ads)Third-party advertising, analytics
Approximate location derived from your IP addressThird-party advertising
Performance and crash diagnostics of the ad SDKAnalytics, app functionality

We do not receive this data and cannot identify you from it. Google's use of it is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. On iOS the app asks for permission to track before the advertising identifier is used; if you decline, ads are not personalised with that identifier. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Buying "Remove Ads" removes the full-screen ads; rewarded ads are always optional.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. The app keeps only a record on your device that a purchase was made, so that it can be restored.

The web version (browser / PWA)

WhatDetails
Progress and settingsStored in your browser's local storage on your device only.
Gameplay statisticsAnonymous gameplay events (for example "level 12 cleared in 4 taps") are kept on your device with a random install identifier that is not linked to you. They are not sent anywhere unless a statistics endpoint is enabled; if we ever enable one, you will be asked first and can decline.
AdvertisingThe web version shows no third-party ads and loads nothing from ad networks.
Challenge linksA link you share contains only the puzzle identifier, your tap count and your tap sequence. It never contains your name or any identifier.

Children

Chainspark is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, and ads are requested with content suitable for a general audience.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's site data, removes everything stored locally. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the choices above or Google's own controls at adssettings.google.com. For any question, write to the address above.

Changes

If what the game or its partners collect changes, this page will be updated first and the app's privacy details on the App Store will be updated to match.
